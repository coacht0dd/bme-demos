COVERALL

The site is open for business, but it has coverage gaps where it counts most. It has a solid base of pages and a clear group-benefits focus. But Google and AI assistants get mixed signals, the site shows no reviews, and Geri herself is almost invisible.

Like a policy with a high deductible: it works, until someone actually needs it to.

01Coverage Schedule

Ten checks, graded the way you'd review a client's policy. Every finding below was checked on the live site on Oct 17, 2026.

#CoverageGradeFinding
01Platform & mobile speedCPARTIAL "You're renting your website, and it advertises the landlord."

The site runs on BrightFire, a rented WordPress platform for agents. The footer carries a BrightFire logo and ad link on every page. You don't own the design, and you can't easily move it.

Mobile speed score: TO CONFIRM (Google's test tool hit its daily limit; we'll rerun before the meeting)
02Schema (Google's data tags)BPARTIAL "Google knows your address, but not when you're open or who you serve."

There's a basic InsuranceAgency tag with your address, phone, and map pin. Good start. But no hours, no service area, and no Service or FAQ tags, so Google has to guess the rest.

03City / location pagesDGAP "You say 'all of Georgia,' but Google only sees Duluth."

There is exactly one location page. It has about 150 words of its own, no map, no reviews, and no FAQ. No pages for Lawrenceville, Suwanee, Johns Creek, Norcross, or any other town an employer might search from.

/locations/duluth-georgia/ is the only location URL in the sitemap
04Service pagesCPARTIAL "Your #1 specialty is competing with itself."

Group benefits, the thing you're known for, lives on two separate sets of pages with the same title. Each tells Google it's the real one, so they split your ranking power. The site also has 70+ template pages, including kidnap & ransom, vineyard & winery, and farm insurance. Are those lines you actually write?

/insurance-services/group-benefits/ and /group-benefits-2/ both titled "Duluth Group Benefits | Insuring Georgia," each with its own canonical
05Headline & page titleCPARTIAL "Your headline and your title tag each tell half the story."

The headline says "specializes in group employee benefits" but no city. The page title says "Insuring Duluth & Georgia" but no service. Google ranks the page best when both appear together, like "Group Employee Benefits in Duluth, GA."

06Reviews on the siteFGAP "Customers can't see one word from a happy client."

The homepage has a "Leave a Review" button but shows zero reviews. Employers and families picking an agent want proof first. Right now, they leave the site to find it, and might not come back.

Google review count & rating: TO CONFIRM from the Google Business Profile
07FAQ / AI-answer readinessCPARTIAL "When someone asks ChatGPT for a benefits agent in Duluth, your site gives it nothing to quote."

There is a FAQ page, but it's buried under Support > Insurance Resources, has no FAQ tags, and isn't on the pages people actually land on. The blog has 40 articles, but they appear to come from the website vendor's content feed, and some repeat (two Medigap posts, two Part D posts).

08Easy contact pathBCOVERED "People can reach you, but the buttons don't give them a reason to."

Click-to-call, a quote form on the homepage, and self-service for billing, claims, certificates, and policy changes. That's real value. The calls to action ("talk to us today") are generic, and there's no way to book a time.

09Trust & credentialsDGAP "Your 'Meet Geri' page is a headshot and a phone number."

No story, no years licensed, no licenses listed, no "why I do this." For an agency where people buy from a person, the person is missing. The Gwinnett Chamber membership and carrier list help, but they're easy to miss.

10Sitemap & indexingBCOVERED "Google can find everything, including a test page."

The sitemaps and robots file are set up. But a page called "Media – Test – Post" is live and listed in your sitemap for Google to index. Small fix, bad first impression.

/media-test-post/ returns 200 and is in post-sitemap.xml · Search Console access: TO CONFIRM

THE OPENING

In Duluth itself, no agency pairs group benefits and Medicare with reviews on the site and answers AI can quote. Brand & Britt does it, but from Grayson. The business owner who also needs help with Mom's Medicare, searching from Duluth or Johns Creek, has no clear local choice. That spot is open.

04The Plan

Today's site

  • Rented platform with a vendor ad in the footer
  • Two competing group-benefits sections
  • One thin city page for "all of Georgia"
  • Zero reviews shown
  • Geri is a name and a phone number
  • FAQ hidden, no AI-ready answers
  • A test page live on Google

What we build (by traffic impact)

  1. One strong group-benefits hub, duplicates merged and redirected
  2. City pages for Duluth and the Gwinnett towns you serve
  3. Headline + title that say service and city together
  4. Real Google reviews shown on every key page
  5. FAQ answers on each service and city page, tagged for Google and AI
  6. A real "Meet Geri" story with photo and credentials
  7. Full Google tags: hours, service area, services, FAQ
  8. Clean-up: test page removed, off-niche pages trimmed

Your domain. Your website. Your content. Update it yourself: just tell the AI what to change.
