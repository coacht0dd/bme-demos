Questions Georgia Employers Ask Us

Don't see yours? Call the Duluth office and ask. We'd rather explain it than have you guess.

Is my business big enough to offer group benefits?
Probably, yes. Many group dental, vision, and voluntary plans are built for small teams, and the rules depend on the carrier and plan type. Tell us your headcount and we'll show you what fits. Call (678) 775-6744.
Do you only sell one insurance company's plans?
No. We're an independent agency, so we quote your plan with multiple carriers and lay the options side by side. We don't work for an insurance company; we work for you. Call (678) 775-6744 to start a comparison.
Will you help my employees during open enrollment?
Yes. We help explain the plan choices to your team and answer their questions, so enrollment questions don't all land on your desk. Call (678) 775-6744 and we'll plan your next enrollment with you.
We already have a plan. Can we still switch to you?
Yes. We can review your current plan any time and compare it with other carriers. Changes usually go smoothest at your renewal date, so it helps to start a few months ahead. Call (678) 775-6744 to set up a review.
Can you also handle our business insurance?
Yes. We also quote commercial property, general liability, and workers' compensation for Georgia businesses, so your benefits and business coverage can sit with one local agency. Call (678) 775-6744 to talk it through.
