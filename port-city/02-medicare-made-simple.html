Your Turning 65 Checklist

Six simple steps. You do not have to figure them out alone.

Medicare feels big because nobody hands you a map. Here is the order we walk Georgia families through, one step at a time.

Helping a parent?

You are welcome to call with questions and set up a time to meet together. Bring your notes. We will keep it simple for both of you.

(678) 775-6744

  1. Know your enrollment window

    Your Initial Enrollment Period is a 7-month window around your 65th birthday month: the 3 months before, your birthday month, and the 3 months after. Signing up on time can help you avoid gaps and late costs.

  2. Decide on Part A and Part B

    These are Original Medicare: Part A for hospital care and Part B for doctor visits and outpatient care. If you are still working with employer coverage, your choices may be different, so ask us first.

  3. Compare Medicare Advantage and Medigap

    Two different paths. Medicare Advantage bundles your coverage into one private plan. Medigap works next to Original Medicare to help with costs it leaves behind. We show you both, in plain words.

  4. Check your prescriptions for Part D

    Make a list of every medicine you take, with the dose. We look up how each one is covered in the drug plans we offer, so there are fewer surprises at the pharmacy.

  5. Review your doctors

    Write down the doctors, specialists, and hospitals you want to keep. Some plans use networks. We check them before you choose, not after.

  6. Call us and talk it through

    Bring your list and your questions. A licensed agent in Duluth will help you compare and decide at your own pace. Call (678) 775-6744.

The Parts of Medicare

The letters, made simple.

Medicare comes in parts. Each one covers something different. Here is what each one means, in one or two sentences.

PartA

Hospital insurance

Helps cover inpatient hospital stays, skilled nursing care, hospice, and some home health care.

Original Medicare
PartB

Medical insurance

Helps cover doctor visits, outpatient care, medical supplies, and many preventive services.

Original Medicare
PartC

Medicare Advantage

A private plan approved by Medicare that bundles Part A and Part B, and often drug coverage, into one plan.

We help with this
PartD

Prescription drugs

Helps pay for the medicines you take. Offered by private plans approved by Medicare.

We help with this
SupplementMedigap

Fills the gaps

Works alongside Original Medicare to help pay costs it does not cover, like some copays and deductibles.

We help with this

Good to know: Medigap works with Original Medicare, not with Medicare Advantage. Most people choose one path or the other. We will help you see which fits your doctors, medicines, and budget.

Common Questions

Questions Georgia Families Ask Us

Don't see yours? There are no silly questions about Medicare. Call us at (678) 775-6744.

When should I sign up for Medicare?

Most people sign up during their Initial Enrollment Period, the 7-month window around the month you turn 65. It starts 3 months before your birthday month and ends 3 months after. If you are still working with employer coverage, your timing may be different. Call (678) 775-6744 and we will help you map out your dates.

What's the difference between Medicare Advantage and a Medigap plan?

Medicare Advantage replaces how you get Original Medicare and often includes drug coverage and a provider network. Medigap works alongside Original Medicare and helps pay costs it leaves behind. You cannot use both at once. We can lay the two side by side for your situation at (678) 775-6744.

Do you only sell one company's plans?

No. We are an independent agency, so we compare plans from the carriers we represent instead of pushing one company. As we like to say, we do not work for an insurance company; we work for you. Call (678) 775-6744 to compare.

Will I be able to keep my doctors and prescriptions?

It depends on the plan, which is why we check before you choose. Bring a list of your doctors and medicines, and we will look them up in the plans we offer in your area. Call (678) 775-6744 to set a time.

I'm helping my mom or dad. Can I call for them?

Yes. You can call us with questions and set up a meeting for your parent. Your parent should be part of the final decision, and you are welcome to sit in. Call (678) 775-6744 and we will walk you both through it.
