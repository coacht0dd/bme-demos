Questions

Questions Georgia Families Ask Us

Don't see yours? Call Geri at (770) 891-2464. There are no silly questions here.

Do you work for one insurance company?

No. We are an independent agency, so we work for you and not for an insurance company. We compare plans from several carriers, such as Nationwide, UnitedHealthcare, and Trustmark, and show you the options side by side. Call Geri at (770) 891-2464 to start.

Can you help my small business set up employee benefits?

Yes. Group employee benefits are our specialty. We help Georgia employers put together group dental, vision, long-term care, and voluntary benefits that fit their team and budget. Call (678) 775-6744 and we will walk you through it.

I'm turning 65. Can you help me make sense of Medicare?

Yes. We explain Medicare Advantage, Part D drug plans, and Medicare Supplement plans in plain English, then help you compare the plans we offer in your area. We do not offer every plan available. Call Geri at (770) 891-2464.

What happens if I need to file a claim?

You call us first. We help you understand what your policy covers, what the carrier needs from you, and what comes next, so you are not doing it alone. Reach Geri directly at (770) 891-2464.

Where is your office and when are you open?

We are at 6340 Sugarloaf Pkwy, Suite 200, in Duluth, Georgia. We are open Monday through Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm, and closed on weekends. Call (678) 775-6744 to set a time to stop by.
