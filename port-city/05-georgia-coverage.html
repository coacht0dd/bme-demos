FAQ

Questions Georgia Families Ask Us

Don't see yours? We're happy to talk it through.

Do you work with businesses outside Duluth?

Yes. Our office is in Duluth, but we help businesses across Gwinnett County and all of Georgia. Most of the work happens by phone, email, and video, and we can meet in person when it helps. Call (678) 775-6744 to get started.

Do I have to come to your office to get a quote?

No. Most quotes are done by phone or email from wherever you are in Georgia. If you would like to sit down face to face, our Duluth office on Sugarloaf Pkwy is open weekdays 9 to 5. Call (678) 775-6744 to set a time.

What does it mean that you're an independent agency?

It means we work for you, not one insurance company. We compare plans from several carriers and show you the options side by side, so you can pick what fits. Call (678) 775-6744 and we'll walk you through it.

Can you help my small company set up employee benefits?

Yes. Group benefits are our specialty. We help Georgia employers set up group dental, vision, long-term care, and voluntary benefits, and we help explain them to your team. Call (678) 775-6744 to talk through your group.

I'm turning 65. Can you help me with Medicare?

Yes. We help you compare Medicare Advantage, Part D drug plans, and Medicare Supplement plans that we offer in your area, in plain English. Call (678) 775-6744 to talk with a licensed agent.
