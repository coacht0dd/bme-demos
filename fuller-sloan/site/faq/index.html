Do I have to wait until I'm sued to call a lawyer?

No. The best time to call is when a deal starts to go sideways. We often settle disputes before a lawsuit is ever filed, through demand letters or mediation. Call us at 770-622-4700 and we'll talk through your options.

What can I do if a partner breaks our agreement?

Start by saving the agreement, emails, and payment records, then call a business litigation lawyer before you act. Many partner disputes settle through a demand letter or mediation. If they do not, we file suit and push the case toward trial. Call 770-622-4700 to review your options.

What if the owner will not pay my draw?

Document the work done, the invoices sent, and every response. Georgia lien and bond rules have strict deadlines, so call a construction attorney quickly. We can send a demand, protect your lien rights, and file suit if needed. Call 770-622-4700.

What should I check before signing a commercial lease?

Look closely at the term, rent increases, repair duties, personal guarantees, and how you can get out early. These terms cost the most when they go wrong. We review leases before you sign. Call 770-622-4700.

What should a business contract include?

At minimum: who does what, when, how payment works, what happens if someone breaks the deal, and how disputes get resolved. Vague terms cause most contract fights. We draft and review contracts in plain terms. Call 770-622-4700.

Do I need a lawyer on retainer?

If you sign contracts, hire employees, or deal with vendors every month, a monthly plan usually costs less than paying hourly each time. It also means problems get caught early. Call 770-622-4700 to see which plan fits.

What should I do after a truck accident?

Get medical care first. Then keep photos, the police report, and every insurance letter, and do not give a recorded statement before talking to a lawyer. Trucking companies move fast to protect themselves. Call 770-622-4700.
