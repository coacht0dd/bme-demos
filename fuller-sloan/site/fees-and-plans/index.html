Questions About Fees

How much does a business lawyer cost in Johns Creek?

It depends on the work. We offer hourly, flat-fee, contingency, and set monthly fees, and we talk cost before you commit. The first consultation is free for litigation and ongoing clients. Call 770-622-4700 for a quote.

Can you be our company's lawyer without a full-time hire?

Yes. Our General Counsel plan gives your business a legal team on call for a set monthly fee. It covers contracts, labor, IP, asset protection, and some litigation. Call 770-622-4700 to see which plan fits.

How is a monthly plan billed?

Plans are billed at a set monthly fee, the same way you budget for insurance. Plan clients also get discounts on larger matters. Call 770-622-4700 for current pricing.
