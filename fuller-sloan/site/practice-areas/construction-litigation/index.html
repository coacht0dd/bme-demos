What if the owner will not pay my draw?

Document the work done, the invoices sent, and every response. Georgia lien and bond rules have strict deadlines, so call a construction attorney quickly. We can send a demand, protect your lien rights, and file suit if needed. Call 770-622-4700.

How does a construction lien work in Georgia?

A lien lets an unpaid contractor or supplier put a claim on the property. Georgia has strict filing and notice deadlines, and a small mistake can void the lien. Talk to us before you file. Call 770-622-4700 and we will check your timeline.

Who is responsible for construction defects?

It depends on the contracts, the plans, and who did the work. Owners, contractors, subcontractors, and designers can all share responsibility. We review the paperwork and the facts to find who owes the repair. Call 770-622-4700 to start.
