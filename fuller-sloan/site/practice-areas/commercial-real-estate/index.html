What should I check before signing a commercial lease?

Look closely at the term, rent increases, repair duties, personal guarantees, and how you can get out early. These terms cost the most when they go wrong. We review leases before you sign. Call 770-622-4700.

What happens if a buyer backs out of a deal?

Your options depend on the purchase contract, especially the earnest money and default terms. Some sellers keep the earnest money; others can sue to close the deal. We review the contract and tell you where you stand. Call 770-622-4700.

Do I need a lawyer to buy a commercial building?

It is strongly recommended. A commercial purchase involves due diligence, title, zoning, financing, and contract terms that are not standard. A lawyer helps you avoid costly surprises. Call 770-622-4700 to talk about your purchase.
