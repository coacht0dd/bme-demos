What can I do if a partner breaks our agreement?

Start by saving the agreement, emails, and payment records, then call a business litigation lawyer before you act. Many partner disputes settle through a demand letter or mediation. If they do not, we file suit and push the case toward trial. Call 770-622-4700 to review your options.

Can I settle before a lawsuit is filed?

Often, yes. A clear demand letter backed by trial preparation resolves many disputes without a lawsuit. We also use mediation when both sides want to keep talking. Call 770-622-4700 and we will tell you honestly whether settlement is realistic.

What happens if my company is sued?

Do not ignore the papers. Georgia sets short deadlines to answer a lawsuit, and missing one can lead to a default judgment. Send us the complaint right away so a partner can review it and protect your deadlines. Call 770-622-4700 today.
