What should I do after a truck accident?

Get medical care first. Then keep photos, the police report, and every insurance letter, and do not give a recorded statement before talking to a lawyer. Trucking companies move fast to protect themselves. Call 770-622-4700.

How long do I have to file an injury claim in Georgia?

Georgia law sets a firm deadline for injury lawsuits, and some claims have even shorter notice rules. Waiting can end your case. Call 770-622-4700 as soon as possible so we can check your deadline.

Who pays my medical bills after an accident?

It depends on the insurance involved: yours, the other driver's, and the trucking company's. We sort out which policies apply and push for payment. Call 770-622-4700 to talk through your situation.
