Donnie Sloan brings an engineer's precision and decades of deal experience to corporate, securities, and real estate work.

Donnie handles venture capital transactions, private placements, securities, corporate law, and commercial real estate. He also advises on contracts, e-commerce, trademarks and copyrights, taxation, and trusts and estates.

He served on the editorial board of the Georgia Law Review before earning an LL.M. from Harvard Law School.
