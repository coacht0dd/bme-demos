Questions Business Owners Ask Us

Do I have to wait until I'm sued to call a lawyer?

No. The best time to call is when a deal starts to go sideways. We often settle disputes before a lawsuit is ever filed, through demand letters or mediation. Call us at 770-622-4700 and we'll talk through your options.

How much does a business lawyer cost in Johns Creek?

It depends on the work. We offer hourly, flat-fee, contingency, and set monthly fees, and we talk cost before you commit. The first consultation is free for litigation and ongoing clients. Call 770-622-4700 for a quote.

Will I actually talk to a partner?

Yes. A partner handles your matter and returns your calls and emails. You won't be handed off to a junior associate you've never met. Call 770-622-4700 to speak with a partner about your case.

Can you be our company's lawyer without a full-time hire?

Yes. Our General Counsel plan gives your business a legal team on call for a set monthly fee. It covers contracts, labor, IP, asset protection, and some litigation. Call 770-622-4700 to see which plan fits.

Do you handle cases outside Johns Creek?

Yes. Our office is in Johns Creek, and we represent businesses across metro Atlanta in Georgia state courts, federal court, and on appeal to the 11th Circuit. Call 770-622-4700 to see if we can help.
