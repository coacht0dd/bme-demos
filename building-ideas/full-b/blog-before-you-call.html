What to Decide Before You Call a Contractor

Five things to think through first, so your first conversation is a useful one.

[Sample post for layout. Building Ideas to review and approve.]

The first call with a contractor goes better when you’ve thought through a few things. You don’t need a full plan. You just need to know what you want and why.

1. What isn’t working today?

Start with the problem, not the solution. “The kitchen is too small for all of us to cook” tells a builder more than “I want a new kitchen.”

2. What does done look like?

Picture the finished space. Write down the three things that matter most. Everything else is negotiable.

3. What budget range feels right?

You don’t need an exact number. A range helps a builder tell you what’s possible and what to put first.

4. When do you need it finished?

Holidays, a new baby, a lease ending. Real deadlines shape the plan, so share them early.

5. Will you live there during the work?

This changes how a job is scheduled and phased. It’s a good question to raise on the first call.

Bring this list to your planning visit

  • Photos of the space and anything you’ve saved for ideas
  • Your budget range
  • Your timeline and any hard deadlines
  • Any drawings, surveys or HOA rules you already have
