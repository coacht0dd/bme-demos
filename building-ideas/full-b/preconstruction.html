What’s Included

Feasibility

Can it be built on this lot, in this house, within these rules? We find out first.

Budgeting

A realistic budget built from the actual scope, not a guess on a napkin.

Scope definition

A written list of what is in the job and what is not, so everyone agrees.

Design coordination

We work with your architect or designer so the drawings and the budget match.

Permit guidance

What permits your project needs and what the process looks like.

Project planning

Sequence and schedule, so you know what happens when.

Why Plan First?

  • Changes on paper cost far less than changes on site
  • You see the real budget before you sign for the build
  • Fewer stops, delays and change orders during construction
  • Your architect’s design gets built, not value-engineered away

What is preconstruction?

Preconstruction is the planning that happens before building starts: feasibility, budget, scope, design coordination and permit guidance. It settles the big questions on paper, where changes are cheap. Call or text 404-771-9004 to ask about your project.

Do I need preconstruction for a small project?

Not always. A planning visit may be enough for a simple job. For whole-home remodels, additions and new homes, preconstruction is where most surprises get caught early.
