Getting started

Do you only remodel, or do you build new homes too?

We do both. Building Ideas remodels and adds on to existing homes and builds new custom, infill and investor homes across North Metro Atlanta. Call or text 404-771-9004 to talk about your project.

Do you work in my area?

We work across North Metro Atlanta, including Johns Creek, North Fulton, Forsyth, Cherokee, Gwinnett and Cobb. Not sure if you are in range? Call or text 404-771-9004 and we will tell you.

How do I know what it will cost before you start?

You get a written scope and budget before any work begins. Our preconstruction step settles design, pricing and permits on paper first, so you are not guessing. Book a planning visit to get started.

What should I have ready for a planning visit?

Photos of the space, ideas you’ve saved, a budget range and your timeline. Drawings, surveys or HOA rules help too if you have them. Book a planning visit to get started.

New homes and development

Do you build custom homes on my lot?

Yes. We build custom homes across North Metro Atlanta. If you already have a lot, we start with feasibility and budget so you know what makes sense before you commit. Call or text 404-771-9004.

What is an infill home?

An infill home is a new house built on an empty or teardown lot inside an established neighborhood. It lets you live where you want in a brand-new home. We can help you check whether a lot works.

Do you build for investors?

Yes. We build investor-owned and spec homes with written budgets and builder discipline, because thin margins can’t absorb surprises. See our Residential Development page or call us.

Planning and permits

What is preconstruction?

Preconstruction is the planning that happens before building starts: feasibility, budget, scope, design coordination and permit guidance. It settles the big questions on paper, where changes are cheap. Call or text 404-771-9004 to ask about your project.

Do I need preconstruction for a small project?

Not always. A planning visit may be enough for a simple job. For whole-home remodels, additions and new homes, preconstruction is where most surprises get caught early.

Can you help with permits?

Yes. Permit guidance is part of our design and preconstruction service, so you know what your project needs before construction starts. Call or text 404-771-9004 to ask about yours.
