Quick answer

Building Ideas partners with investors and landowners on spec homes, infill development and small residential projects in North Metro Atlanta. We review feasibility and budget with you before anyone commits capital. Call or text 404-771-9004 to discuss an opportunity.

What We Take On

Spec home development

New homes built for sale, with builder discipline on cost from the first estimate.

Infill development

Teardown and empty lots in established neighborhoods, reviewed for feasibility first.

Boutique residential projects

Small groups of homes where quality and fit with the area matter.

Joint ventures

Partnerships with investors and landowners where each side brings what it does best.

How We Approach a Deal

  1. Site and numbers review

    Feasibility, rough budget and what the market supports.

  2. Preconstruction

    Written scope, budget and schedule before capital is committed.

  3. Build and report

    We manage the build and keep partners informed on cost and progress.
