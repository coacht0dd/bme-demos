How We Work

One clear path from the first call to the final walkthrough. You always know what comes next.

  1. First conversation

    Call, text or fill out the form. We ask a few questions to make sure we’re the right fit.

  2. Planning visit

    We walk your home or lot, listen, and tell you what’s possible.

  3. Preconstruction

    Feasibility, written scope, budget, design coordination and permit guidance.

  4. Contract and handoff

    We agree on scope, price and schedule. Your project manager takes it from here.

  5. Project planning

    Schedule, trades and materials are lined up before work starts.

  6. Build

    We manage the job site and keep it clean and organized.

  7. Budget tracking

    We track actual costs against the plan as the job moves. [Confirm how often clients get updates]

  8. Closeout

    Final walkthrough, punch list and the keys.

  9. After the job

    We stay in touch. Most of our work comes from people we’ve built for before.
