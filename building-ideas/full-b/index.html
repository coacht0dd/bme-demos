Questions Homeowners Ask Us

See all questions →
Do you only remodel, or do you build new homes too?

We do both. Building Ideas remodels and adds on to existing homes and builds new custom, infill and investor homes across North Metro Atlanta. Call or text 404-771-9004 to talk about your project.

Do you work in my area?

We work across North Metro Atlanta, including Johns Creek, North Fulton, Forsyth, Cherokee, Gwinnett and Cobb. Not sure if you are in range? Call or text 404-771-9004 and we will tell you.

How do I know what it will cost before you start?

You get a written scope and budget before any work begins. Our preconstruction step settles design, pricing and permits on paper first, so you are not guessing. Book a planning visit to get started.

Can you help with permits?

Yes. Permit guidance is part of our design and preconstruction service, so you know what your project needs before construction starts. Call or text 404-771-9004 to ask about yours.

Who will I talk to during my project?

You will have one point of contact from our family team for the whole job. Founder Jair Contreras leads every project relationship. Call or text 404-771-9004 to meet us.
