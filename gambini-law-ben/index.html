Before You Begin

What's the difference between a will and a trust?

A will says who gets your assets, but it usually goes through probate. A trust can hold your assets, often skip probate, and keep things private. Which is right depends on your family — that's exactly what the Blueprint Session sorts out.

How do I protect my aging parents' assets?

Proactive elder law planning — powers of attorney, care directives, and Medicaid planning — protects both their assets and their dignity. The earlier we plan, the more options stay open.

What is the Strategic Legacy Blueprint Session?

It's a focused $200 working session. We assess your family and assets, find the gaps and risks, and give you a custom action plan — plus time to ask an attorney anything. You leave with a clear path forward.
